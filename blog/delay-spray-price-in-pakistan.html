Original vs Fake: Don't Let a Low Price Fool You

One reason the delay spray price in Pakistan varies so much is counterfeits. Fake products are commonly sold at suspiciously low prices — sometimes half the market rate. A genuine product comes factory-sealed, with clear labelling, a readable expiry date, and consistent packaging. If a seller cannot show real product photos, avoids questions about authenticity, or offers no return support, walk away no matter how tempting the price looks.

The safest approach is to buy from an established online store that specialises in men's personal care, displays transparent pricing, and has a real customer support channel. Paying the fair market price for an original is always better than saving a few hundred rupees on a fake.
