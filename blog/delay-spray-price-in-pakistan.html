Why the Same Product Costs Different on Different Sites

This confuses everyone, so let me break it down. First, brand and origin. A recognised imported name like Viga 50000 Delay Spray carries import duties, shipping, and brand premium baked into its price. An unbranded local bottle doesn't. That's not automatically a reason to pick one over the other — it's just why the numbers differ.

Second, bottle size. This one trips people up constantly. A PKR 1,500 bottle that holds 45ml is a better deal than a PKR 1,200 bottle holding 20ml. Do the per-ml math before you decide something is "cheap." The Viga Spray 45ml and Viga One Million Strong Spray are good examples — the larger format looks pricier at first glance but stretches much further.

Third, bundles. Almost every serious seller discounts multi-bottle packs. Two or three bottles together nearly always beat the single-bottle price per unit. If you already know which product you like, buying the bundle is the easiest money you'll ever save.

And fourth — the one nobody talks about — who you're buying from. A dedicated personal-care store that imports directly skips the middlemen and can price lower than a general marketplace where three resellers each take a cut. It also tends to mean fresher stock, which matters more than you'd think for any personal care product.

The Fake Problem Nobody Warns You About

Here's the uncomfortable truth behind a lot of the price confusion: a big chunk of the cheapest listings are counterfeit. The delay spray category is one of the most faked personal-care segments in Pakistan. You'll see bottles at half the market rate on random social media pages, and they look convincing in photos.

So how do you protect yourself? Real products come factory-sealed with crisp labelling and a readable expiry date. The seller shows actual product photos — not stock images stolen from somewhere else. They answer questions. They offer cash on delivery, because COD means you can inspect the parcel before paying. A seller who dodges questions, has no return support, and only takes advance payment? That's your answer right there.

My rule is simple: if a price is dramatically below the normal delay spray price in Pakistan for that brand, it's not a bargain. It's a fake. Pay the fair price once instead of paying twice — first for the fake, then for the real thing.
