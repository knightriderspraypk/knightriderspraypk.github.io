Condom Price in Pakistan: Brand-by-Brand Breakdown

The condoms pakistan market splits pretty neatly into three tiers. At the bottom sit the mass-market local brands — Sathi, Josh, Touch — which most medical stores and pharmacies stock. The middle tier is where you find upgraded local lines like Condo Max, Climax, Coronet Gold, and Bang. The top tier belongs to imported or premium-labelled brands such as Carex, Happy Life, and Aroma Plus.

Sathi condom price in Pakistan typically starts around PKR 300–450 for a pack of 12. Sathi has been around for years and is probably the most recognised name on pharmacy shelves. It's a no-frills option — plain or lightly lubricated latex — and that's exactly why it's popular: it's cheap, available everywhere, and does the job.

Josh condom price in Pakistan lands in a similar band, roughly PKR 350–500 per 12-pack depending on the variant. Josh runs a wider range than most local brands — dotted, flavoured, and delay variants — so prices vary a bit by pack. Touch condom price in Pakistan also sits in this budget zone, usually PKR 300–450.

Move one rung up and you're looking at happy life condom packs around PKR 1,299–1,499. Happy Life positions itself as a premium import with extra lubrication and a snug dotted texture. Carex sits nearby: the Carex dotted assorted flavour pack usually retails around PKR 1,299. What you're paying for here is variety — twelve pieces, multiple flavours, dotted texture — and packaging that actually feels like a real brand product.

If you've ever searched for silicone condoms price in pakistan, note that "silicone" is usually a mix-up: nearly all condoms are natural latex (some premium ones are polyurethane). Genuine latex premium packs still fall in that PKR 900–1,800 band; anything labelled "silicone" at a tiny price is likely mislabeled, so buy from a store that lists the actual material.

Why Prices Vary So Much

Most buyers are surprised the first time they compare. A pack that costs PKR 350 at a corner pharmacy might be listed at PKR 1,500 online under a different brand — and both can be legitimate. The gap comes down to a few things:

  • Imported vs local: imports carry duties and shipping, so they cost more. Local brands keep overhead low.
  • Texture & features: dotted, ribbed, flavoured, and extra-lubricated variants always cost more than plain ones. The Carex multi-pleasure pack combines dotted and ribbed textures, which is why it sits above plain Carex.
  • Pack size: 3-packs look cheap but cost more per piece; 12-packs and 30-packs bring the per-unit price down. The DO 3-in-1 passion 30-pack is built exactly for buyers who want bulk value.
  • Where you buy: online stores with cash on delivery often undercut pharmacy shelves because there's no retail markup. Just make sure the store is legit.

Buying Condoms Online in Pakistan: What to Check

Most buyers I talk to still hesitate about ordering condoms online, and honestly the hesitation makes sense — this is a private purchase. Here's what matters: cash on delivery so you pay when the parcel arrives, and discreet packaging — plain, unmarked parcels with no product name on the outside. Any store worth ordering from offers both without you having to ask.

Beyond that, check three things before you pay. First, real product photos — stock images from Google are a red flag. Second, a clear return or replacement policy for damaged packs. Third, reviews from actual buyers in Pakistan, not generic five-star spam. If a store lists the condom price clearly on the product page with no "DM for price" games, that's usually a good sign too.

One more practical tip: expiry dates. Latex condoms typically have a shelf life of a few years, but check the date on the box when it arrives, especially with bulk 30-packs that you'll be storing a while. Store them somewhere cool and dry — a wallet back pocket for weeks on end is the fastest way to ruin good latex.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average condom price in Pakistan?
Local brands like Sathi, Josh, and Touch cost roughly PKR 300–500 per 12-pack. Mid-range textured brands run PKR 800–1,200, and premium imported-style packs like Happy Life or Carex go for PKR 1,200–1,800.

Which is the cheapest condom in Pakistan?
Among recognised brands, Sathi and Touch are usually the cheapest per pack. Buying 12-packs or larger bundles always beats buying small 3-packs on per-piece cost.

How can I spot fake condoms?
Watch for prices far below market rate, blurry or generic packaging, no expiry date, and sellers who won't show real product photos. Buy from established online stores with COD and discreet packaging.

Is it safe to order condoms online in Pakistan?
Yes — as long as the store ships in plain, unmarked parcels and offers cash on delivery. Check reviews and make sure prices are listed openly on the product pages.

Do dotted condoms cost more than plain ones?
Generally yes. Texture, flavouring, and extra lubrication all add a small premium — usually a few hundred rupees more per pack compared with the same brand's plain version.
