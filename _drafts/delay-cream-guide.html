What Is Delay Cream?

Delay cream is a topical personal-care product for men, designed to reduce sensitivity and help you last longer during intimacy. It's the cream-format cousin of delay sprays. You apply a small amount, wait for it to absorb, wipe off the excess, and go about your night. Wash it off afterwards.

Nothing about it is complicated or medical. It's a grooming product, plain and simple — the same category as the 100 Minutes Delay Cream, which is one of the most searched delay creams in Pakistan right now. Men buy it for the same reason they buy any performance-oriented personal care: confidence.

The Main Types of Delay Cream You'll Find in Pakistan

Not all delay creams are the same. Broadly, the Pakistani market has three types, and knowing which is which saves you from buying the wrong thing:

  • Standard delay creams — the everyday option. Mild desensitising effect, easy to use, suited to most men. The 4X Delay Cream for Men sits in this lane, marketed for up to 90 minutes of performance.
  • Extra-power delay creams — stronger formulas with bolder duration claims. Black Cobra Delay Cream, advertised for up to 180 minutes, is the classic example. These are for experienced users — start with a tiny amount.
  • Herbal delay creams — plant-based ingredient blends for men who prefer to avoid synthetic desensitisers. Gentler positioning, popular with first-time buyers who feel cautious.

There's no universally "best" type. A first-timer is usually happier with a standard cream, while someone who already knows their tolerance might reach for an extra-power option. Be honest about which one you are.

How to Use Delay Cream Correctly

Most disappointment with delay creams comes from using them wrong, not from the product itself. Get these basics right:

  • Apply 15–20 minutes before. The cream needs time to absorb into the skin. Applying it two minutes before intimacy and wondering why nothing happened is the #1 rookie mistake.
  • Less is more. Start with a pea-sized amount. You can increase slightly next time if needed.
  • Massage, don't smear. Work it in evenly so absorption is uniform.
  • Wipe off excess before intimacy. A damp tissue does the job. This prevents transfer and keeps the effect where it belongs.
  • Wash afterwards. Soap and water, and you're back to normal.

Do a solo trial run first. Seriously. Learn how much you need and how your body reacts when there's zero pressure. Future you will be grateful.

How to Spot a Fake Delay Cream

Counterfeits are the real problem in this market, not the products themselves. Here's how to protect yourself:

  • Check the price. Way below market rate = fake. There are no exceptions to this.
  • Look for a real store. Genuine sellers have proper product pages with photos, descriptions, and prices — not a blurry marketplace thumbnail.
  • Inspect the packaging on arrival. Blurry printing, misspelt brand names, broken seals — all red flags. Don't use it; contact the seller.
  • Avoid "too good" bundles. Five famous-brand creams for the price of one is not a sale. It's a scam.
