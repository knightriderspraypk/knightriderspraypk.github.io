How to Use These Creams Safely

A few practical ground rules, framed as grooming common sense. First, patch-test: apply a small amount to your inner forearm and wait a few hours. Any redness or itching means that product isn't for you — this applies to every cream on this list. Second, follow the usage directions on the packaging; more is not better with any personal-care product. Third, these are external-use grooming products — keep them away from eyes and broken skin, and store them somewhere cool. None of this is dramatic; it's the same care you'd give any new skincare product.
