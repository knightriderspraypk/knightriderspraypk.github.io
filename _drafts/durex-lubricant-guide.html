What Is Durex Lubricant & What Is It Used For?

Durex lubricant uses are broader than most people realise. Yes, it's for intimacy — reducing friction and dryness, which makes things more comfortable for both partners. How to Use Lubricant: A Few Practical Tips

  • Match the base to your condoms. Water-based lubes (all the Durex Play gels and Carex jellies above) are safe with latex condoms. Oil-based products can weaken latex — keep those for massage only.
  • Less is more at first. Start with a small amount and add as needed. You can always add more; you can't easily take it away.
  • Patch test sensation lubes. If you have sensitive skin, try a dab of Tingle, warming, or cooling formulas on your inner arm first.
  • Store it right. Keep bottles sealed, away from direct sunlight and heat — the same rule as condoms.
  • Check the expiry date. Lube doesn't last forever. An expired bottle isn't worth the risk when a fresh one costs the same.
