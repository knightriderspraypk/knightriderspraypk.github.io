Red Flags: How to Spot Fake Products

  • Guaranteed size claims — "add 3 inches in 30 days" is marketing fiction, not product information. Legitimate sellers don't promise results.
  • No fixed price — sellers who only quote prices "in inbox" are usually adjusting them based on what they think you'll pay.
  • Stock photos only — a real store shows the actual bottle or tube from multiple angles, including the seal and labelling.
  • No COD — if a seller won't take cash on delivery, they're not confident you'll want to pay after seeing the product.

How to Use Safely (General Guidance)

These are external-use products only. Do a patch test first — a small amount on your inner forearm, wait 24 hours, and check for irritation before any regular use. Use only the amount the product's own packaging suggests, keep it away from eyes and broken skin, and stop immediately if you notice redness, itching, or discomfort. Store the bottle or tube somewhere cool and dry, and always check the expiry date on sealed packaging before opening. This is general personal-care guidance, not medical advice — if irritation doesn't clear up, see a pharmacist or doctor.
