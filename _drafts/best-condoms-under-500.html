Why Are These So Cheap? The 3-Pack Economics

A 12-pack from a premium brand costs ₨1,000+ because you're paying for brand marketing, import costs, and a bigger box. A 3-pack from a local brand skips most of that. The latex itself doesn't cost much — what you're really paying for in a budget pack is the manufacturing, the lubricant, and the packaging. Brands like Dabbang, DO, Bang, and Aroma Plus make condoms in volume for the local market, so a 3-pack can sell for ₨135–₨255 and still be a properly sealed, lubricated, electronically tested condom. The math works out to roughly ₨45–₨85 per condom, which is genuinely hard to beat.

That said, price per condom isn't the whole story. A 3-pack is also the smartest way to try a new texture or brand without committing to a dozen — if you hate dotted condoms, better to find out for ₨145 than for ₨1,499.

Cheap Doesn't Mean Expired: The Quality Checklist

The one legitimate worry about budget condoms is quality control, so here's how to protect yourself regardless of price. First, check the expiry date on the box — never buy condoms without one, and never use expired ones; old latex degrades and can break. Second, the foil should be sealed flat with no air puffiness or tears. Third, buy from a store that stores stock properly (heat is latex's enemy) and shows real product photos.
