Getting the Most Out of Your Bottle

Whichever spray you pick, technique matters more than the label. Apply 2–3 light sprays 10–15 minutes before, let the product absorb fully, and wipe off any excess before contact. That's it — but it's the step most disappointing reviews skip. Spraying too late or using half the bottle in one go is how men end up blaming the product for what was really an application problem.

First-time users should start at the low end: two sprays, one occasion, then adjust. Every man's sensitivity differs, and the "right" dose for you is something you discover, not something a label can tell you. A bottle used this way lasts a long time — at 2–3 sprays per use, the per-use cost of even the strongest pick under 2,000 stays remarkably low, which is another reason this budget band is such good value.
