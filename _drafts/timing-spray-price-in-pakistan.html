What Moves the Timing Spray Price in Pakistan?

Three things, mainly. Origin comes first — imported products like the Viga range carry duties and shipping that local bottles don't. That's why Viga One Million Strong Spray and Viga 8 Million Delay Spray sit higher on the shelf than homegrown options. You're paying partly for the name and partly for the import chain.

Bottle size is second, and as I said above, it's where the real value hides. The Viga Spray 45ml looks expensive next to a small local bottle until you realise it holds more than twice the product.

The seller is third and the most underrated. A dedicated personal-care store that imports directly will almost always beat a general marketplace where the product has passed through two or three resellers. Fewer middlemen, fresher stock, better price — and usually better support if something goes wrong with your order.

The Counterfeit Trap (Read This Before You Buy Cheap)

Here's the dark side of the timing spray price in Pakistan: the cheapest listings are very often fake. This category is one of the most counterfeited personal-care segments in the country. You'll find bottles at half the market rate on random Facebook pages and WhatsApp sellers, and the photos look convincing.

Genuine products have tells: factory-sealed caps, crisp printing, a readable expiry date. The seller shows real photos of the actual product, answers questions directly, and offers cash on delivery — because COD lets you inspect the parcel before paying, which is the last thing a counterfeiter wants. A seller who only takes advance payment, dodges questions about authenticity, and has no reachable support? That's all the information you need.

My advice hasn't changed: if the timing spray price in Pakistan for a known brand is suddenly half everywhere else's, it's not a sale. It's a fake. The Durex Long Time Delay Spray is a good example of honest mid-range pricing — a global name, locally made, priced fairly instead of suspiciously.
