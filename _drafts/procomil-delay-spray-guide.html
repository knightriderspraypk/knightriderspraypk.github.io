How to Use Procomil Spray (Step by Step)

This is the part most guides rush through, so let's do it properly:

  1. Shake the bottle lightly before use.
  2. Apply 2–3 sprays from a short distance. First-timers: start with 2. You can adjust next time.
  3. Wait 10–15 minutes. This is the step people skip, and it's the whole ballgame. Give it the full time.
  4. Wipe off excess with a tissue before getting started. Cleaner and more comfortable.
  5. Store properly. Cap on, room temperature, out of direct sunlight.

The most common beginner mistake is spraying too much and not waiting long enough — which is exactly backwards. Fewer sprays, full wait time. Once you've done it two or three times, you'll know your own number.
