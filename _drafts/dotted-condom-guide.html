What Are Dotted Condoms?

A dotted condom is a regular latex condom with raised studs or dots across its surface. The dots add texture, which many couples say increases sensation for both partners. They come in the same standard shapes and sizes as regular condoms — the only difference is that textured finish. In Pakistan, dotted condoms are by far the most popular texture, ahead of ribbed ones, and local brands like Carex, Climax, Coronet Gold and Condo Max all sell dotted versions. If you've only ever used plain condoms, a dotted one is the easiest upgrade to try: no learning curve, no extra steps, just a different feel.

Types of Dotted Condoms in Pakistan

Most buyers assume "dotted" is one thing. It's actually four different products, and mixing them up leads to disappointing purchases.

Plain dotted. The classic: raised studs, no flavour, no delay effect. Condo Max's dotted 12-pack is the budget workhorse here, while Climax dotted condoms offer a deeper, more noticeable stud pattern. Either is a solid default choice.

Dotted + flavoured. Same studs, plus flavours like strawberry, banana, or chocolate. The Carex assorted flavour dotted pack gives you four flavours in 12 condoms, which is the cheapest way to figure out which flavour you actually like. For smaller trial packs, Aroma Plus apple flavour dotted (3 pack) and Bang lubricated dotted (3 pack) are handy low-commitment options.

Dotted + ribbed combos. Dots on the shaft, ribs near the tip — double texture. The Carex Multi Pleasure 12-pack is the best-known version in Pakistan, and if you want the full sampler experience, the DO 3-in-1 Passion Pack bundles ribbed, dotted and delay variants together.

Dotted + timing (delay). These are dotted condoms with a mild delay lubricant, designed for men who want to last longer. Timing condoms are a huge search term for a reason — they work simply, with no extra products needed. The Carex Delay 12-pack and Amor Long Love delay condoms are the two dotted timing options buyers reorder most.
