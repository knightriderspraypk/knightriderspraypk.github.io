Viga Spray Types: Decoding the Numbers

The numbers on Viga bottles — 50000, 150000, 1 Million — refer to the strength/concentration of the delay formula. Higher number, stronger effect. Here's the lineup you'll actually encounter in Pakistan:

Viga 50000. The original and still the best starting point. The Viga 50000 delay spray (45ml) is the mildest of the range, which makes it the right choice for first-time buyers. Most men don't need the stronger versions — starting here and seeing how you respond is the smart move. Around PKR 2,499 for 45ml.

Viga 1 Million / One Million. A step up in strength from the classic. The Viga One Million strong spray (~PKR 2,799) suits men who tried 50000 and found it too mild. Don't jump straight here as a beginner — the stronger formula means less margin for error on dosage.

Viga 8 Million. Near the top of the strength range sold in Pakistan. Viga 8 Million (45ml) at around PKR 2,999 is for experienced users who know their tolerance. More is not better with delay sprays — too much and you'll numb yourself past the point of enjoyment.

Viga Spray 45ml (standard). You'll also see a plain Viga spray 45ml listing (~PKR 2,299) — usually the standard formula in the classic bottle. When in doubt between similarly-named listings, check the product photos and description carefully, or ask the seller which strength it is before ordering.

How to Use Viga Spray Properly

Most complaints about delay sprays come from wrong usage, not bad products. The method: apply 2–3 sprays to the sensitive areas about 10–15 minutes before intimacy, then wait. The waiting matters — the formula needs time to absorb, and wiping off any excess before starting is standard practice. Start with fewer sprays than you think you need; you can always add one more next time, but you can't un-spray. And patch-test on your forearm first if you have sensitive skin — the same rule as any new personal-care product.
