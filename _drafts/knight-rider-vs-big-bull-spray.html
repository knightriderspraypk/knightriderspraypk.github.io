How to Use Either Spray Properly

Whichever you choose, the method is the same — and it's what separates good results from disappointing ones:

  1. Apply 2–3 light sprays to the most sensitive area 10–15 minutes before.
  2. Let it absorb fully; wipe off any excess before contact.
  3. Start with fewer sprays on your first use and adjust next time.

Most "it didn't work" complaints come from applying too late or using too much. Less is more, especially with a stronger product like Big Bull.
