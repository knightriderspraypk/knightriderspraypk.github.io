How to Spot Originals (Especially at Budget Prices)

The cheaper the product, the more important this section becomes. Counterfeit condoms cluster at the bottom of the price range because that's where buyers are least likely to question a deal. Protect yourself with a few simple checks:

  • Buy from real stores: a proper online shop with real product photos, clear PKR pricing and cash on delivery. No COD option is a yellow flag.
  • Check the foil: sealed edges, clear printing, an expiry date that's actually in the future. Blurry printing or missing dates — don't use them.
  • Price sanity check: if a Happy Life pack is selling at half the normal budget price, something's off. Even budget brands have a floor.
  • Smell and feel: fresh latex has a distinct but clean smell. A strong chemical or rancid odour means the pack is compromised — throw it out.
