The Fake Problem (Read This Before You Buy Cheap)

I'll be blunt: fake condoms are everywhere in Pakistan's grey market, and they're dangerous in a way a fake phone case isn't. Counterfeiters target Durex specifically because the brand name sells itself. They list at half the market rate on unverified pages, and buyers chasing the lowest Durex condoms price in Pakistan walk straight into it.

Genuine packs: factory-sealed, crisp printing, readable expiry date on every pack, sold by stores with real photos and real support. Fakes: blurry packaging, no verifiable seller, prices that make no sense, and — almost always — no cash on delivery option. That last one is telling. COD lets you inspect before paying, which is exactly what scammers don't want.
