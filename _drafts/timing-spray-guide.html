What Exactly Is a Timing Spray?

A timing spray is a topical personal-care spray for men, packaged in a small pocket-sized bottle. You apply a small amount before intimacy, wait a short while, and the product's desensitizing formula (most brands use lidocaine or benzocaine-based formulas in the personal-care category) temporarily reduces sensitivity. That's the entire idea: less sensitivity, more control, a more relaxed experience.

Most sprays on the Pakistani market come in 12cc–45ml bottles, which last anywhere from a few weeks to a couple of months depending on how often you use them. The 45ml bottles — like the Vimax 45ml or Viga 45ml — tend to be better value per use than tiny bottles, even though the upfront price is higher.

How to Use a Timing Spray (Getting It Right)

Most first-timers use too much — that ruins the experience, not improves it. Here's the routine that works for nearly every brand:

  • Start with 1–2 sprays on the most sensitive area, about 10–15 minutes before intimacy.
  • Let it absorb fully; wipe off any excess before contact. This step matters more than people think.
  • See how you respond before increasing. Most users never need more than 3 sprays.
  • Do a small patch test the first time, as you would with any new personal-care product.

A 45ml bottle used at 2 sprays per session typically lasts well over a hundred applications — which is why bigger bottles are the better buy for regular users.
