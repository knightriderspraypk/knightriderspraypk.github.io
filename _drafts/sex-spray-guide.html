What Is a Sex Spray, Really?

Strip away the marketing and a sex spray is a mild topical spray for external use. You apply a couple of sprays to the most sensitive area about 10–15 minutes before intimacy, let it absorb, and wipe off any excess. The idea is straightforward: a little reduced sensitivity means longer performance. That's the whole concept — no pills, no equipment, nothing complicated.

The most searched names in Pakistan are Eros Delay Spray, the Viga range, Knight Rider, and Vimax. They all work on the same basic principle, differing mainly in strength, bottle size, and price. None of these are medicines, and treating them as personal-care products — not pharmaceutical fixes — is the honest way to look at them.

How to Use a Timing Spray Properly

Most first-time disappointments come from using the product wrong, not from the product itself. The method is simple: shake the bottle, spray 2–3 times on the sensitive area, and wait 10–15 minutes. That waiting period matters — it's when the product absorbs. Wipe away any excess with a damp cloth before intimacy.

Start with fewer sprays than you think you need. Two is plenty for a first try. You can always add one more next time; you can't un-spray. And do a small patch test on your first use — a single spray on the forearm, wait ten minutes, check for irritation. Sensible, takes a minute, saves you trouble.
