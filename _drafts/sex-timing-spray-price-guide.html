Why Prices Vary So Much

Three things drive the price of a timing spray: origin (local vs. imported), bottle size (22cc vs. 45ml makes a real per-use difference), and authenticity (originals cost more than fakes — obviously, but the fakes don't advertise that they're fakes). A fourth factor is the seller's overhead: a proper store with a website, COD logistics, and customer support charges a fair listed price, while a WhatsApp-only dealer might undercut on price and overdeliver on risk.

How to Spot Fakes (Save Your Money)

Counterfeit timing sprays are everywhere in Pakistan — possibly the majority of what's sold through informal channels. Here's how to protect yourself:

  • Too-cheap pricing: A "Knight Rider" listed at PKR 600 isn't a deal; it's a fake. Compare against the real price bands above.
  • No website, only WhatsApp: Legitimate sellers invest in a real store. If the entire business is a WhatsApp number and some forwarded photos, walk away.
  • Advance payment demands: Cash on delivery exists precisely so you don't pay before seeing the product. Anyone insisting on advance payment for a personal-care item is a risk.
  • Stock photos only: Real sellers photograph their actual inventory. Stolen or generic images are a classic fake-seller tell.
  • No clear return/exchange policy: A store that stands behind originals will say so plainly.
