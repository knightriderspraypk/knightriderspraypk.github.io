How to Use Knight Rider Cream: Step by Step

"How to use Knight Rider cream" is one of the most searched questions about this product, and the answer is simple — but the details matter. Here's the routine:

  • Step 1 — Time it right. Apply 15 to 20 minutes before intimacy. The cream needs that window to absorb. Last-minute application is the most common reason people say "it didn't work."
  • Step 2 — Small amount. Squeeze out a pea-sized dab of Knight Rider Delay Cream. That's your starting dose. More is not better — too much just numbs everything.
  • Step 3 — Massage it in. Gently work the cream into the skin until it's mostly absorbed. Even coverage means even effect.
  • Step 4 — Wipe off the excess. Before intimacy, wipe away any remaining residue with a tissue or damp cloth. This step is non-negotiable: it keeps the effect on you and prevents any transfer.
  • Step 5 — Wash up after. Soap and water when you're done. Sensation returns to normal on its own.

First-timer advice: do a trial run on your own a day or two before you actually need it. You'll learn exactly how much your body needs and how long the effect takes to kick in for you. Walking into the real moment already knowing your dose changes everything.
