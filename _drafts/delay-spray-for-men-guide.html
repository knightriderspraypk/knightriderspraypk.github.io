How to Choose: The 5 Things That Actually Matter

1. Strength — start mild. This is where most buyers go wrong: they grab the strongest bottle on the shelf. Stronger isn't better; it's just stronger. If you're new, start with a mild-to-medium option like Knight Rider or Vimax 45ml. Only consider heavy-hitters like Viga 8 Million or Viga One Million once you know how your body responds to milder formulas.

2. Bottle size — think per-use cost. A 45ml bottle at PKR 1,800 sounds expensive next to a small bottle at PKR 999, but the big bottle delivers several times more applications. The Viga 45ml and Viga 50000 are the classic examples: higher upfront, far cheaper per use. If you're only trying the category once, a small or budget bottle like Big Bull makes sense; if you'll use it regularly, buy big.

3. Formula type — spray vs cream. Sprays absorb fast and are easy to dose. Creams, like the Knight Rider delay cream, suit buyers who dislike the spray format or want more control over exactly where product goes. Same category, different texture — pick whichever you'll actually use correctly.

4. Brand track record. In a market full of no-name bottles, stick to names with a history: Knight Rider, Viga, Vimax. These brands have been around long enough that fakes are easier to spot (you know what the real bottle looks like) and buyer reviews actually exist.

5. Seller trust. The product is only half the purchase; the other half is the seller. Browse the delay spray category of a specialist store and check: real photos, clear volumes, open pricing, cash on delivery, discreet packaging. If any of those are missing, keep looking.

How to Use Delay Spray Properly

Technique matters more than brand. Here's the routine that experienced users follow, regardless of which bottle they buy:

  • Less is more. Start with 1–2 sprays. You can always add; you can't subtract.
  • Timing: apply 10–15 minutes before intimacy so the formula absorbs.
  • Wipe the excess. Once absorbed, gently wipe off residue before contact. Skipping this step is the #1 beginner mistake.
  • Patch test first. Try a small amount on less sensitive skin a day before, like you would with any new personal-care product.
  • Don't mix with other products on the same area in the same session — keep it simple while you learn your response.

Users who complain "it didn't work" usually applied too much, too late, or on top of other products. Get the routine right and even a mid-range bottle performs well.

Avoiding Fakes: A Quick Checklist

Delay sprays are among the most counterfeited personal-care products in Pakistan. Before you order, run through this:

  • Product photos are real, not stock images — and the bottle matches the brand's standard packaging.
  • The volume (ml/cc) is stated clearly on the listing.
  • The store offers cash on delivery and ships in plain, unmarked parcels.
  • There are genuine buyer reviews mentioning the product by name.
