How to Use It

The routine is the same as with any timing spray:

  • Shake the bottle and apply 2–3 sprays to the sensitive area, about 15 minutes before intimacy.
  • Wait for it to absorb fully. Don't rinse.
  • First-timers: start with 2 sprays. You can increase to 3 next time if needed — you can't take it back once applied.
  • Store it at room temperature, away from direct sunlight.
