How Men Use Horse Power Cream

The routine is the same one that applies to virtually every timing cream on the market:

  • Apply 15–20 minutes before intimacy. The cream needs time to absorb — rushing this step is why most "it didn't work" complaints happen.
  • Use a small amount. A pea-sized dab is the standard starting point. Adjust upward only if needed, never downward from a whole handful.
  • Massage it in evenly so absorption is uniform across the skin.
  • Wipe off any excess with a tissue before intimacy. Don't skip this.
  • Wash with soap and water afterwards. Normal sensation returns by itself.

As with any new personal-care product, do a trial run solo first. Learn your ideal amount with zero pressure, and you'll walk into the real thing confident instead of guessing.

How to Spot Fake Horse Power Cream

Affordable + popular = heavily counterfeited. Here's how to make sure the tube you get is genuine Horse Power Cream:

  • Price check. Around PKR 999 is the real price. A PKR 300 "Horse Power" is not a deal — it's a fake.
  • Buy from a specialist. General marketplaces are where fakes thrive. A dedicated men's-care store with real product pages and clear pricing is far safer.
  • Inspect on arrival. Sharp printing, correct brand spelling, sealed packaging. Blurry labels or broken seals mean don't use it — contact the seller instead.
  • Check the seller's catalogue. A genuine store sells the full range — MM3 Cream, Golden H Cream, the sprays, the lot. Fly-by-night sellers list one suspicious product and nothing else.
