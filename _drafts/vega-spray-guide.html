Vega vs Viga: What's the Difference?

Vega is a brand name most commonly associated with tablets (the well-known "Vega 100" sildenafil tablets). When a delay spray is sold under a Vega-style name in Pakistan, it's usually a local or imported spray product trading on the familiar name — for example Vega 990000 Spray (Germany).

Viga, on the other hand, is the established family of delay sprays sold in Pakistan — the 45ml bottles with names like Viga Spray 45ml, Viga 1 Million Strong, and Viga 8 Million Super Strong. When your friend says "that Viga spray worked for me," this is the family he means.

So here's the practical rule: if you searched "vega spray" but you want a timing/delay spray, compare the Viga range — that's almost certainly what you're after. If a seller shows you a "Vega spray," check the actual product photos and label before paying. The name on the listing and the name on the bottle should match.

How to Use a Viga-Type Spray

The method is the same across the family: 2–3 sprays on the sensitive area, 10–15 minutes of waiting while it absorbs, wipe off excess. With the stronger bottles (1 Million, 8 Million), start at the low end — two sprays, not four. Stronger formulas punish over-application with numbness that kills the mood instead of extending it. First-timers: do a patch test on your forearm and wait ten minutes before full use. It takes a minute and it's the responsible move.
