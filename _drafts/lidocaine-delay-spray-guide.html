How to Use It

Same drill as most quality sprays, with one extra emphasis — because lidocaine works through desensitizing, the timing matters even more:

  • Shake the can lightly, apply 2–3 sprays from a short distance.
  • Wait the full 10–15 minutes. Don't rush this — the lidocaine needs the time to work.
  • Wipe off any excess with a tissue before getting started.
  • Start with fewer sprays your first time and find your own number over a couple of uses.

One tip specific to lidocaine sprays: wiping off the excess after the wait is especially worth doing here, so everything stays comfortable for your partner too.
