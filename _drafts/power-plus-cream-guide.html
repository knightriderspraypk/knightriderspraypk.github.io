How to Use It

Usage follows the standard routine for timing creams:

  • Apply a small, pea-sized amount about 10–15 minutes beforehand.
  • Use a thin, even layer — piling it on doesn't improve anything.
  • Wash your hands thoroughly after applying.
  • Patch-test on a small area first, especially if you have sensitive skin.
  • Store the tube in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

One practical tip: because it's a cream rather than a spray, take the extra minute to let it absorb properly. Rushing this step is the most common beginner mistake I hear about — with creams, patience is part of the method.
