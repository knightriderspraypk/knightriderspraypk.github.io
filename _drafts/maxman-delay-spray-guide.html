What Is Maxman Delay Spray?

Maxman Delay Spray (sold as Maxman 75000) is a men's personal care spray designed to help extend performance time. You apply a few sprays, wait a short while, and it helps you stay in control longer. It's one of the better-known names in the delay spray category in Pakistan, sitting right alongside heavyweights like Knight Rider and Big Bull.

What I like about it: it does one thing and keeps the instructions simple. No complicated routine, no weird preparation. Spray, wait, carry on. That matters, because honestly, half the products in this space make things harder than they need to be.

How to Use Maxman Spray

The method is basically the same across most quality sprays, and Maxman is no exception. Shake the bottle lightly. Apply 2–3 sprays from a short distance, roughly 10–15 minutes before you're ready. Then wait. That waiting window is the part people skip, and it's the part that makes the whole thing work.

A couple of practical tips from experience: start with fewer sprays your first time. You can always add one more next time, but you can't un-spray. And wipe off any excess with a tissue after the waiting period — this keeps things tidy and comfortable for both people involved. Store the bottle at room temperature, cap on, away from direct sunlight, and it'll last you a good while since one bottle goes through a lot of uses.
